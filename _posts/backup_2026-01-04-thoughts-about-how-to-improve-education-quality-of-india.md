Noting down my thoughts about the challenges if one wants to contribute to improvement of education quality in India, especially in the more underprivileged regions.

### Introduction

- The analogy of country with a single human being, and the people of the country to cells of the body.
- Its not easy to deny the fact that education is one of the fastest way to take a country towards general betterment. Better educated people are probably likely to be less corrupt too (`check`).
- Hence, in order to improve country -> improve its education system. Surely this is true for India, where there is enormous scope of improvement in the education system.

### How to improve Education quality

- Definitely lack of money is an issue. This is because it is not profitable to put money in that, because the people don't have much money to begin with.
- Even if we have a lot of money, where to put it for max improvement in education quality is a difficult question to answer.
- So value to optimize is improvement in quality of education. But we need to define quality of education first.
- Definitely there would be many indices for it (`check`). Some parameters on which it can be parametrized would be subjective grading of kids responses to questions from that school. The questions would be related to science, general curiosity, etc, with the goal to understand grasp of student's understanding, and general satisfaction (how much they are enjoying learning and coming to school), etc. (`check and refer to gpt`). Students should be sampled randomly from the given school. 
- First of all, in which state/region/area/school to put the money is not trivial. One possible idea that can help in this is to give all schools a 'credit-score' (`check if something like this already exists. atleast a database of all schools`). This credit score should ideally be a measure of how well the school responds to funding, because that is the value we want to optimize. However, to know how a school responds to funding, we need to give it funding in the first place. Hence, it is a bit tricky initially as there are so many schools. A proxy for initial credit score could be just the current quality of eduction as defined above of that school. This is under the assumption that a school (in rural area, as we are only talking about those schools here) with higher quality of education is likely to also make better use of more incoming funding. As more and more schools are funded over time, we get more data about how they respond to funding, and the credit score estimate improves. However, this also means that every now and then, the funding should be redirected to the schools which haven't been funded till now to give them a chance to show that they can make good use of the money too. 
- The previous point highlights the importance of definition of quality of education, as everything is determined by that.
- Once the credit score of schools is established/determined, then the schools with higher credit scores can be chosen more frequently for funding. Next question is: for the chosen school, where should that funding go?
- Some options are (`check in gpt too`): i) improving infrastructure, like new benches, chairs, toilet cleaning etc. ii) finding better teachers, or training the existing teachers. iii) one unique idea would be to take some of their own students, train them for maybe a year in education, and get them to work in the same school as teachers. This way, their own person will there in the village to teach, but trained likely better than the existing teachers. This cycle can maybe repeat also. Top students from the school can be paid to stay after their studies and teach in the same school. iv) funding the organization supporting this whole framework, which ideally shouldn't require much money.
- It should be noted that everything that happened with the funder's money should be kept super transparent. So for example they contributed to benches, the pic of the benches should be clicked and sent to them. If they chose to fund a teacher, then they should be put in contact with that teacher and they can choose to ask the teacher for regular updates, as they are funding that teacher. The school they chose, its credit report should be given to the funder regularly.
- One more thing to investigate in this is that how possible it is rule-wise to hire a teacher by a different organization (and salary given by a different organization) into an already existing govt school (`check`)? Also `check` about the prevalence of private schools in villages.
